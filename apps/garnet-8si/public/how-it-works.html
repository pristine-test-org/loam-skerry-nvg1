How Garnet works

Garnet keeps one shared list for the whole household, and the front door shows the five newest entries the moment you arrive.

  1. Add an entryAnyone with the link can put a new item on the shared list.
  2. See it at onceThe front door reads the list live, so nothing needs refreshing by hand.
  3. Tick it offFinished items stay visible with a check mark until they scroll away.

Most lists are around two fifths done by mid-week.
